Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Memora moves your Snapchat Memories into Apple Photos. It runs entirely on your iPhone.

The short version: Memora collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no servers of ours. Your photos, videos, dates and locations never leave your device.

What Memora accesses, and why

Your Snapchat export file

You choose it with the iOS file picker. Memora reads it to find your Memories and their dates and locations. It is never uploaded, copied off the device, or sent anywhere.

Your photo library

Memora needs permission to add your Memories to Apple Photos, and to read back what it added so it can verify the import finished correctly. It does not browse, index, or transmit the rest of your library.

Snapchat's download servers

If your export contains download links rather than the media itself, Memora fetches those files directly from Snapchat, over HTTPS, using the links Snapchat gave you. Nothing else is sent with those requests, and the files go straight to your device.

What stays on your device

  • A record of which Memories have been imported, so an interrupted import can resume and duplicates are not created
  • Your import settings and history reports
  • Temporary copies of media while an import is in progress, deleted afterwards

All of it lives in Memora's own app storage. Deleting the app removes all of it.

What Memora does not do

  • No analytics or crash-tracking SDKs that profile you
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data to sell
  • No Snapchat login, password, or account access
  • No Snapchat private APIs
  • No accounts and no sign-in of any kind

Purchases

If you buy the Lifetime Unlock, it is a one-time In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple. Apple processes the payment; Memora never sees your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, which is why it can be restored on a new device.

iCloud

If you have iCloud Photos enabled, Apple will sync your imported Memories to iCloud the same way it syncs everything else in your library. That is between you and Apple — it is your device setting, not something Memora controls or can see.

Children

Memora is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because Memora holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on our side. Everything is on your device and under your control. Deleting the app deletes everything Memora stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
