What Memora does

Memora reads an official data export that you have requested from Snapchat, and copies the Memories inside it into your Apple Photos library, preserving their original capture dates and locations where that information is present in the export.

Memora does not connect to your Snapchat account. It has no login, requests no credentials, and uses no Snapchat private interfaces. It only reads the export file that you supply and, where the export contains download links instead of the media itself, fetches those files from the addresses Snapchat placed in your export.
