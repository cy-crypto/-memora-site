Support

Memora puts your Snapchat Memories into Apple Photos, with their real dates and places. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Need a hand? Email abdullah@creamole.de and describe what you were doing when it went wrong. If the import produced a report, the summary at the top of it tells me most of what I need.

Getting your export from Snapchat

Memora needs the official export that Snapchat gives you. It never asks for your Snapchat password.

  • In Snapchat, open Settings → My Data.
  • Tick Export your Memories, and make sure Export JSON Files is also ticked — without the JSON, the dates and locations are missing and there is nothing for Memora to restore.
  • Submit the request. Snapchat emails you a download link, usually within a few hours but sometimes a day or more.
  • Download the ZIP on your iPhone and save it to Files.

Common questions

Do I need to log in to Snapchat?

No. Memora has no login of any kind. It only reads the export file you give it.

My export came as several ZIP files.

That is normal for large accounts. Select all of the parts together — Memora treats them as one export and will tell you if a part is missing.

Some Memories came in with today's date.

That happens when the export has no JSON file, or when a particular Memory has no date recorded in it. Memora never invents a date: if the export does not say when a photo was taken, the photo keeps whatever date Photos gives it. Re-requesting the export with Export JSON Files ticked usually fixes it.

Some Memories failed to import.

The most common cause is an expired download link — Snapchat's links stop working after a while, so an old export may no longer be able to fetch its media. Request a fresh export. The import report names every Memory that did not make it and why.

Can I run it again without creating duplicates?

Yes. Memora remembers what it has already imported and skips it, so an interrupted import can be resumed safely.

Will this delete anything from Snapchat?

No. Memora only reads your export file. It never contacts your Snapchat account and cannot change anything there.

Where did my Memories go?

Into your Apple Photos library, dated to when they were taken — so they appear in the right place in your timeline, not all at the end. If you allowed full photo access, they are also collected in an album.
